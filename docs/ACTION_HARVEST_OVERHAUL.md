# RIPE RIOT — Action Harvest Overhaul

25 September 2026. This is the active Sunpatch design and implementation scope.
The original [game design](../GAME_DESIGN.md) remains a record of the fruit,
physics, and visual systems that still work. The co-op action harvest is now the
route through them.

## Player promise

Explore a bright, unruly island with friends; fight fruit and plants that fight
back; secure valuable harvests; and take down the King Vine to extract the King
Melon. Physical fruit handling remains a source of choices and comedy, while
combat and shared recovery drive the first route. The polygonal island and its
existing fruit, shop, tools, and physics are retained.

## Sunpatch route

1. From the dock, follow the worn path to the orchard. Ordinary fruit can be
   picked and sold, but the Mimic Melon charges after a visible warning. The
   starter Picking Mallet can strike it. A defeat banks a base reward and drops
   a physical prize that can be secured for more.
2. The hill path passes Snapjaw. It warns before biting, holds one player
   briefly, and can be drawn off by thrown fruit. The victim holds E to pry
   free; a nearby teammate presses E to release them.
3. The climb introduces the Spitter Plant. It marks a firing lane and launches
   an arcing shot that can be dodged. The Air Cannon is a tier-zero purchase
   priced for the early harvest, damages the plant from range, and can deflect
   its shot. Melee remains possible up close.
4. King Vine guards the King Melon at the ravine. A sweep or seed attack has a
   warning and a recovery window. Players hit the exposed stem with the Mallet
   or Air Cannon, or return a seed with the Air Cannon. Its defeat unlocks vine
   cutting.
5. Cut four holding vines and guide the falling King Melon to its extraction
   pad. Ropes can steady the drop and earn a bonus but are optional. A no-rope
   drop lands within a short recovery haul. Extraction pays and opens the next
   island boat route.

The current goal stays visible in the HUD. Reward money is banked once by the
host on enemy defeat; the physical prize is an additional risk and opportunity.
The original tool and fruit systems are still available for creative solutions.

## Health, failure, and recovery

Players have 100 health. Enemy and boss hits are decided by the host. A downed
solo player gets one short self-recovery; the next down evacuates them to the
dock and forfeits unsecured fruit. In co-op, a downed player has a 15-second
bleedout; a teammate can hold E nearby for three seconds to revive them. A
successful revival keeps the haul. Banked money and bought tools survive
evacuation. Physical knockdown remains separate from health and does not itself
charge a penalty.

The ravine has walkable southbound exits from both lower pockets. Holding H for
1.25 seconds moves a stuck, active player to verified ground without charging a
death penalty. It preserves held fruit and money and releases only ropes that
would become taut across that relocation. A downed player cannot use it to
bypass the revive/failure state.

## Controls and cooperation

WASD moves, mouse aims, left click uses the selected tool, E interacts and
handles captures/revives, 1–4 selects tools, and H is the stuck fallback. The
Mallet is equipped at the start; the Air Cannon is available early at the shed.
Co-op damage, enemy hits, captures, rewards, boss state, and vine cutting are
host-owned. Clients send bounded hit and rescue intents; snapshots show their
teammates the same encounter phases and projectiles. A local player's own
health and revive progress remain responsive on their machine after the host
authorizes a hit or revive attempt.

## Verification and remaining review

Focused model tests cover warnings, damage, capture, escape, rescue, projectile
deflection, boss hits, solo/co-op failure, and stale snapshots. Headless game
scenarios cover the starter weapon, Air Cannon, route objective, capture, boss
input, optional ropes, no-rope drop, ravine exits, and recovery. Two actual
browser pages verify host-approved damage, enemy and boss hits, and revival in
both directions. Gameplay-camera review frames are generated by
`node tools/harness/action-review.mjs`.

The capture set is a first visual pass, not player art approval. Hardware frame
rate, human route clarity, and wall occlusion for the Spitter projectile still
need hands-on review. The current encounter attempt resets on a fresh load;
defeat milestones and purchases persist through the existing save system.
