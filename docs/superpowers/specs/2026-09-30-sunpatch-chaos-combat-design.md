# Sunpatch Chaos Combat Design

## Intent

The user wants a visible change in how RIPE RIOT plays. The Puff Melon's moving, wind-driven escape is the reference: valuable harvesting should create understandable physical trouble, and fighting should be about redirecting that trouble and recovering the harvest. This work continues the approved Sunpatch expedition at `c5e2aaa`; it does not replace the route, bright rounded voxel direction, existing enemies, mallet timing, $110 Air Cannon, optional ropes, banked money, or saved dock ending.

The first release of this design changes **two encounters in the normal expedition**, not just debug fixtures: the orchard Mimic becomes a fruit-scattering wrecking ball, and Snapjaw eats then flings a player unless the crew intervenes. A local agitation rhythm connects valuable actions to warnings and bursts of trouble. Later Spitter and King Vine changes can use the same impact and rescue rules, but their new attacks are outside this release.

## Playable experience

1. The safe dock and ordinary apple harvest stay calm. The overloaded orchard crop shows a tempting prize and nearby Puff Melons. A first deliberate harvest action visibly warns; a distinct second action releases the authored prize and wakes the Mimic, as the current site ledger already requires.
2. The Mimic commits to a readable charge. Contact launches the player a bounded distance and scatters nearby loose fruit. It can hit a Puff Melon and send it inflating across the clearing. A dodge that leads the charge into a real orchard tree makes the tree drop fruit and briefly stuns the Mimic. The tree collision is a physical-world result, not a random drop on every missed charge.
3. The mallet remains a timed, reliable strike. A recovery hit interrupts and redirects the Mimic's next heading, making the mallet useful for control; it does not gain a longer damage combo or inflated enemy health.
4. Snapjaw's bite visibly holds a player, aims, winds up, and throws them toward another active teammate if one is in a safe direction. Solo throws use a dry, clear landing within the encounter area. A victim can use the existing escape input; a teammate can pull them free, strike the open jaw, blast it with the Air Cannon, or catch the thrown teammate with a timed Catch Net swing. A missed catch still permits recovery and must not mean automatic death.
5. Valuable or violent harvest actions raise local agitation. The warning uses root movement, rustle, and a short cue. A threshold starts a nearby authored windfall or threat response after a tell; a quiet period follows a burst. The current rush order remains optional and must not obscure a danger cue. Ordinary fruit and idle movement do not silently generate danger.

## Mechanical boundaries

### Mimic and fruit

- The host computes charge movement, collision category, impact position and a numbered impact event. It geometrically sweeps authoritative fruit positions along the charge path, including attached fruit whose colliders are not active when the host is far away. An affected attached fruit detaches once before receiving its impulse. A tree impact may detach a small, deterministic subset of attached ordinary fruit through the authoritative FruitSystem API; a fence/rock collision cannot masquerade as a tree, and the authored watermelon prize is excluded from incidental tree shower.
- Add an authored Puff Melon plant to the orchard after current deterministic plant/fruit generation so existing IDs and RNG rolls remain stable. It is a physical, harvestable fruit, not a decorative explosion. Register its IDs in the existing site release/consume save ledger without making Puff harvesting itself trigger the Mimic's two-action gate. A charge only affects it when the swept path actually reaches it. Old saves create it attached; new saves restore attached, launched, or consumed state without duplication.
- A hit to a player carries a host-chosen horizontal impulse and limited vertical lift. The old single-hit-per-charge guard remains. Reduce direct damage as needed so the combined hit and fall are recoverable; prevent repeated knockdowns from one impact. The Mimic still stops short of the camera and returns home without healing or repeating payout.
- Colliding with an ordinary fruit tree during a charge produces a distinct, longer stun/opening and a visible fruit shower. A fence or rock still stops the charge but cannot masquerade as a fruit tree; returning home cannot trigger a shower. A mallet interruption may bias the next charge direction but cannot teleport, permanently stun, or leave the enemy outside its leash. That pending direction is included in encounter state for host promotion.

### Snapjaw and player motion

- Keep the bite telegraph, front cone, bait, protected closed jaw, and two-hit health. Bite damage plus fling landing must be bounded. Capture state has a replicated aim/wind-up timer and one numbered fling decision; escape, teammate rescue, defeat, and fling are mutually exclusive releases.
- Clear `captured` before applying the host-authored launch velocity. During flight the player has a short bounded control restriction, then regains steering. Reuse or extract the existing ragdoll safe-ground checks for the landing target: dry terrain, safe slope, clear capsule, inside the encounter clearing, with a fallback that does not send the player off a cliff or into the sea.
- The host validates remote escape/rescue/net-catch requests against victim identity, encounter generation, input phase, equipped tool, range and timing. A remote Catch Net swing first records a numbered swing start against host time; a later catch intent names that swing and supplies bounded aim. Client assertions of an active window are not authoritative. A one-shot launch/catch result is sequenced and deduplicated; ordinary snapshots carry the ongoing pose/phase for joining peers and host migration. A launch packet clears local capture until an equal or newer authoritative snapshot arrives, so a delayed captured snapshot cannot relock the victim. A successful catch confirms a velocity stop to the victim's client. Do not let a client invent a launch, reward or rescue.
- The thrown player remains visible to teammates: while held, the remote avatar is attached to a clear jaw pose instead of being rendered as downed at its old packet position, then transitions to a flying avatar. The jaw, aim tell, victim marker, launch cue, net catch and landing each need feedback in the gameplay camera. A nonlethal teammate strike or Air Cannon hit during the open-jaw window interrupts the hold. A captured player's HUD states their own escape action; teammates see rescue/catch guidance. Local menus cannot stop shared co-op simulation.
- If bite damage downs the victim, release into the existing downed/revive path and do not fling. Otherwise, clear captured before launching with a non-ragdolling initial impulse, grant brief target immunity at the jaw, and allow only a bounded landing tumble/recovery. The host must not start a second bite while the victim is still escaping the first.

### Agitation and pacing

- Use the existing IslandDirector as the scheduling owner. Track bounded, local pressure for the orchard/hill route from **host-accepted** actions: site disturbance, rare fruit detach, tree shaker, Air Cannon blast near fruit, and tensioned vine release. The authority seam assigns an action ID so client-side cues cannot add phantom or duplicate pressure. Decay pressure during calm play; don't trigger more than one burst at a time or during the King Melon extraction/return.
- At the warning threshold, nearby vegetation visibly stirs and a positional audio cue plays. At the burst threshold, select an existing eligible fruit group for a telegraphed windfall. If no eligible group is present, pressure decays rather than spawning an invisible hazard. Preserve rush-order payout/side-event saves and keep the dock safe.
- Replace the old automatic first-pick windfall: an ordinary apple alone does not start a timer toward trouble. The authored Mimic site supplies the opening chaos even on a fresh save. Because IslandDirector has one event slot, agitation pressure and its pending warning wait through an active rush order; the order keeps its payout and timer, and one burst may start after it ends. Pressure then cools down rather than stacking hidden events.

## State, saves and networking

The host owns encounter phases, impact IDs, fruit detach/impulse and agitation. Snapshots carry durable phase, active capture, pending Mimic redirection, and local pressure needed for failover. One-shot effects use a host identity/epoch plus monotonic sequence ID, are ignored when stale across host promotion, and are not replayed on load. Existing saves without new fields get calm defaults. Reloaded defeated enemies remain defeated, consumed site prizes remain consumed, and the settled expedition never pays twice. Solo uses the same rule path without transport.

## Acceptance evidence

- Model/unit checks: Mimic tree-versus-fence collision, fruit sweep, one impulse per charge, Puff Melon launch, charge interruption, agitation threshold/decay, capture/escape/rescue/fling exclusivity, safe landing and finite velocities.
- Browser and two-client checks: ordinary controls, host-authored launch and fruit state, victim and teammate cues, net catch timing, late/stale packets, host migration, death/revival and save/reload. Existing route, payout and extraction regressions must still pass.
- Gameplay-scale captures at the normal ultrawide aspect and an ordinary-input run must show the orchard fruit disaster and the Snapjaw throw in context. A numeric impulse or isolated pose does not establish that the game feels different. Record what remains unverified, especially human pacing, comedic impact and physical-device play.
