# Sunpatch first-session playtest

Audience validation has not happened yet. Passing automated checks does not establish that the island is funny, polished, or commercially compelling.

## Five independent first sessions

Recruit five people who have not played or watched this version. Record their consent to recording separately. Use a fresh save and the same build for all five. Let each play for at least ten uninterrupted minutes; do not demonstrate a crisis or explain the gull first.

Opening prompt: “This is a game about harvesting fruit and buying equipment. Play as you normally would. You can stop whenever you want.”

Avoid coaching unless the player is technically blocked. Log interventions and exclude those moments from unaided-comprehension results. Do not ask whether a particular event was funny while they play.

Record arrival, first pick, first sale, first purchase, first event warning, any recovery, gull contact, order outcome, and whether they identify the King Melon objective. Note any unreadable signs, apparently floating objects, missed warnings, audio discomfort, or input obstruction.

After play, ask in this order:

1. What were you trying to do when you stopped?
2. What do you think you would do next?
3. What moments do you remember?
4. Was anything confusing or frustrating?
5. Would you choose to keep playing? Why?

Only after those answers, ask about specific events or characters. Distinguish spontaneous incident recall from prompted responses. Capture exact phrasing rather than converting every response to a positive sentiment.

## Separate acceptance evidence

- Automated gameplay/network checks: validate technical rules and recovery.
- Authored camera captures: validate presentation at the captured poses.
- Ten-minute normal-input recording: validates one observed route and sequence; debug event fixtures do not substitute for it.
- Five-minute 1080p RTX 4070 Ti timing run: collect individual frame durations after warmup, report p50/p95/p99, slow-frame counts, exact renderer and concurrent workloads. Target p95 <=16.7 ms. Run when it will not compete with the user's active game.
